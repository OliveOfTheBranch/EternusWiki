###### City
Sigil is a magical city that orbits [[Carceri]]. It is also known as the City of Doors. The city is ruled by [[The Court of Pain]].

It was once located in [[Alfheim]], but was lifted up from the ground by the Court of Pain after they gained power in the second agon. Sigil was one of the two magic cities, alongside [[Arkas]], and was targeted by [[The Triad]] after [[Agon#The Second Agon|The Second Agon]]. Fortunately, Arkas was attacked first, and Sigil had time to lift up, causing the destruction headed its way to fall upon the ground beneath it, creating [[Landfall]].
