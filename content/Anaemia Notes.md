## Misc
Xylemia was the only one who survived the nectar because [[Sanguis]] inhabited her and spared her life. Without a haemmangel, the process of acclimating to the mutagen kills whoever takes it.

In certain private studies of the Ichor mutagen (perhaps done by Matryoshka), it exhibits an ability known as Confluence, where the individual cells are able to interface with the divine via an unknown method.
