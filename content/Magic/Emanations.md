Emanations are strange magical effects where one [[Planes of Existence|Plane]] bleeds into the material as a form of merging. Such areas are incredibly unstable, and often difficult to navigate without being shunted into the oppressing plane.
#### The Lucidity
The Lucidity is a large area and magical phenomenon in [[Alfheim]] that causes the material plane to bleed into the [[Feywild]]. As the Feywild is formed by the light of [[Celestia]] reflecting off of the material plane, the Lucidity acts as a way to move through the mirror and into the reflection, as the light of Celestia is most concentrated there.
#### The Divinity
The Lucidity's opposite is the Divinity, an area in the [[Shining Sea]] where the light of [[Celestia]] is concentrated enough that people can move to the plane through it.
#### The Obscurity
Another such effect is the Obscurity, located in [[the Pitch]], which is said to be a stable gate to [[Oblivia]]. There is not much known about the Obscurity, as the journey to it is perilous and often one-way.
#### The Nihility
The Nihility is located at the north pole of [[Eternus]]. It is where the [[Veil]] becomes extremely close to the material plane, so much so that it becomes thin enough for immaterial souls and entities from the [[Outer Realm]] to move through. [[Arcadia]] is placed above the Nihility, such that souls can pass to the afterlife peacefully.
