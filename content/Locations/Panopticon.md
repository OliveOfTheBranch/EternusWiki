###### Orrery
The Panopticon, also known as Skypiercer, is a tower in [[Arkas]] that stretches infinitely into the sky. It was created by [[Aureon Mentis]] before [[Agon#The First Agon\|The First Agon]], and is the place that he retired to after his daughter, [[Riparia Mentis]], was born. The Panopticon is mentioned in the [[Predator's Bible]] as the place that [[Clio Sara]] journeyed to find the Oracle of Sara.
