###### (she/her) - NPC
Sanguis, or the Maiden, is one of the [[Haemmangels]] and the leader of the [[Valkyries]] in [[Dreygaard]]ian mythology. She is also the psychopomp of their religion, said to be the force which returns blood to the [[Haemloch]].

When [[The Sanguine Lord]] died, she went insane, and forced her hosts to kill themselves by bloodletting.

Sanguis is commonly depicted as an angel holding a [[Blood Lilies|Blood Lily]] in one hand and a spear in the other, surrounded by the fallen and wounded.
