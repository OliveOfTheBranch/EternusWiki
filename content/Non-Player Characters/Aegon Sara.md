###### (he/him) - NPC
Aegon Sara was the son of [[Clio Sara]] and [[Ulysses Sara]]. He was the second ruler of the [[Sara Dominion]], taking the throne after his mother's 50 year rule. He was named after the event that freed his country, and began a trend for future monarchs crowned after an [[Agon]], such as Aegon II and [[Anastasia Sara|Aegon III]].
