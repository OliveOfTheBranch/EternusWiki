###### (she/her) - NPC
Khaos Rosik is a [[Tiefling]] from the [[Impious Alliance]], and is the current Prime Lady of [[Mædway]]. She is the current head of [[House Rosik]], as daughter of [[Sol Rosik]]. Her sister and captain of her guard is [[Harmonia Rosik]].

She was a member of the adventuring party [[Zhetrics]].
