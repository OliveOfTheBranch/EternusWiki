###### (she/her) - NPC
The primordial goddess of nature and the fey, Titania is the queen of the [[Feywild]] and previously the [[Seelie Fey|Seelie]] Queen. After [[Mab]], the [[Unseelie Fey|Unseelie]] Queen, was murdered by [[Lolth]], Titania attempted to unite the two courts under one United Crown. Now, she is a member of [[The Triad]], consisting of herself, [[Pölark]], and [[Lathander]]. As the queen of the Feywild, she is also the Archfey of [[Archheart]].

Titania usually takes the form of a fey with dark brown skin, wearing a flowing dress gilded with gold.
