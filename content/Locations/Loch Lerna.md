###### Lake
Loch Lerna is a large lake in the central [[Sara Dominion]]. It is named after [[Saint Lerna]], who slayed the [[Creatures/Chimaera (Creatures)|Chimaera]] general [[Aegis]] that lived within it. Originally, the lake was named Loch Aegis after this chimaera.
