###### (he/him) - NPC
Xander Mentis was a human from the [[Sara Dominion]]. Nestor’s two sons, Xander and [[Atlas Mentis]], were raised in [[Arkas]] just before [[Agon#The Second Agon\|The Second Agon]]. Xander was a promising young oracle, who was overcome by visions while under the night sky. During his time as oracle, he had a recurring vision of stars falling into a gaping dark mouth. Arkas was obliterated by [[The Triad]] during Xander’s reign, killing both brothers.

Xander had visions while under the night sky. 

He was oracle from 811 GA - 0 BA (4 years).
