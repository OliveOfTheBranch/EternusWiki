###### Pantheon
The Morteus Gods are a subset of [[The Twelve Who Came Before]], comprising of the gods who held dominion over the lives, and more often deaths, of mortals. These gods were:
- [[The Sanguine Lord]]
- [[Octavian]]
- [[Typhon]]
- [[The Wanderer]]

The Morteus Gods were often the most direct with their influence, sometimes even appearing as an avatar to wreak destruction (especially in the case of Typhon).
