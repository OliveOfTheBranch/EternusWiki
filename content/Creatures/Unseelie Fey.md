###### Fey
Unseelie fey are a type, or court, of [[Fey]] that were created by or aligned themselves with [[Mab]] as their ruler. They are typically seen as more cruel and chaotic than their [[Seelie Fey|Seelie]] counterparts, though in reality this isn't always the case. Unseelie fey include, but are not limited to, the following types of fey:
- [[Drow]]
- [[Shadar'kai]]
- [[Hags]]
- [[Darklings]]
- [[Redcaps]]
- [[Peatborn]]
- [[Brigganocks]]
- [[Hedgekin]]
- [[Waxers]]
- Autumn and Winter [[Eladrin]]

After [[Mab]]'s death in the [[Agon#The Second Agon|The Second Agon]], the Unseelie Court disbanded and their capital city, [[Ravenfall|Ravenheart]], was cast into the [[Abyss]] by [[Lolth]]. A few unseelie [[Archfey]] went on to join the United Court of [[Feywild]] being formed by [[Titania]], thought most chose to find their own way.
