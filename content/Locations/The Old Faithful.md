A bar in the Downtown "Belly" District of [[Chimaera]]. Its proprietor is [[Daenerys Fletcher]]. The Old Faithful's basement parlour is occasionally used to host events for [[House Orion]], usually arranged by [[Charity Orion]]. [[Xenevieve Mentis-Sara]] and [[Theodore Sara]] were known to frequent the bar, likely due to Daenerys dating Xenevieve's housekeeper, [[Cypress of Saturnalia]].
