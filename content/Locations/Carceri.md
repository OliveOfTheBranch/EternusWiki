###### Plane
Carceri is a realm, visible in the sky of [[Eternus]] Primordia as a strange black shape. It is used solely by [[The Court of Pain]], and is the location of the [[Godflow Engine]], used to store [[The Wanderer]]'s [[Animus]]. Carceri is watched over by [[Carter of Marwin]], one of the court’s members, who serves as a warden to some of the unfortunate enemies of the court that have been held prisoner there.
