Daymora Khagan was the first of the [[Sun Drakes]] to ever be created by [[Lathander]]. He was also the largest sun drake to have ever existed, being almost twice the size of [[Sol]], his successor. He was slain by an [[Elemental Titans\|Elemental Titan]].
