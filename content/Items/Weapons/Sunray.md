###### Sword
Sunray is a shortsword once belonging to [[Pataras Mentis]]. While few descriptions of the sword have survived to the [[Current Age]], we know that it was gold handled, and had some sort of fire related enchantment, though magic at this time was still in its early stages.

When Pataras died in 145 GA, Sunray was stored in the [[Shrike's Tine]] by his sister, [[Rei Mentis]]. [[Adonis Mentis]] later adopted the sword, taking it with him to [[Regulus]], where it was passed down to his son, [[Elio Mentis]], and later, [[Iris Mentis]]. It was briefly considered lost after [[Agon#The Second Agon|The Second Agon]], though was eventually located in the Shrike's Tine.
