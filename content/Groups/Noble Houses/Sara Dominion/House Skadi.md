###### Noble House
House Skadi are the wardens of the North in the [[Sara Dominion]]. They are descended from [[Sankta Skadi]].

Their family artefact is the [[Collar of Orthus]]. The current acting head of house is [[Öntýna Skadi]], who is in charge while her parents and baby sister live in [[Dreygaard#Südris|Südris]].

House Skadi is based in [[Öndur]], after [[Xenevieve Mentis-Sara]] annexed it back from [[Dreygaard]]. For many years prior to that they were based in [[Chimaera]] at the Dreygaardian embassy.

Their symbol is a cerberus, and their words are "The snow covers all."
