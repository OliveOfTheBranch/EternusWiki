###### (he/it) - NPC
The god of cruelty and resurrection, Orcus is both a deity and a [[Demonlord]] of the [[Abyss]]. Seeking immortality during the [[Agon#The First Agon\|First Agon]], Orcus fought [[The Shepherd]] who charged itself with guarding the barrier between life and death. The fight left Orcus weakened, however, he managed to obtain the Shepherd's [[Animus]] and ascend to godhood. In doing so, the [[Veil]] was left unguarded, making arcane magic and resurrection possible, and inadvertently starting the [[Golden Age]].
