###### (she/it) - NPC
Bavlorna Blightstraw was one of the three [[Hags]] that took over the realm of [[Prismeer]] after imprisoning its [[Archfey]], [[Tasha|Zybilna]]. Bavlorna seized the swampy region, [[Prismeer|Hither]], as her own, taking up residence near the town of Downfall, before she was eventually killed by the adventuring party [[Eternus Victoria]].
