###### Continent
Alfheim is the eastern continent of Eternus. Western Alfheim is home to the Free Cities of the [[Impious Alliance]], including [[Emperia]], [[Mædway]], and [[Faeleen]]. Meanwhile, eastern Alfheim is considered the east edge of the world, as the materiality of the continent begins to blend into the [[Feywild]].

East Alfheim was once the location of the great mage city of [[Sigil]], before it ascended to the [[Astral Plane]] to orbit [[Carceri]] after [[Agon#The Second Agon|The Second Agon]]. As it flew away, [[Pölark]] unleashed the rage of [[The Triad]] upon it, missing the city but creating a sprawling black desert across east Alfheim known as [[Landfall]]. From this desert came hordes of [[Nightshades]] created by Pölark, which upon finding no Sigil in sight instead wrought havoc upon the rest of the continent, becoming one of the main issues during the [[Time of Strife]].
