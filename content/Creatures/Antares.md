###### Chimaera General
Antares was a [[Creatures/Chimaera (Creatures|Chimaera]]%5C) general in the Hunting Grounds of East [[Ethrys]], and took the form of a scorpion. Unlike other chimaera of its time, Antares did not hunt in the traditional fashion, and instead lured its prey into the ancient city of [[Archela]] in the south of what is now modern day [[Sara Dominion]]. It used its mind-altering venom to keep the citizens of Archela under its control, maintaining a steady population and enjoying easy prey without fear of being hunted. Eventually it was defeated by [[Saint Orion]], who later joined the [[Hunters of Sara]], and its tail was utilised for making the Spear of Antares.
