###### (she/her) - NPC
Choler, or the Warrior, is one of the [[Haemmangels]] in [[Dreygaard]]ian mythology. She is praised both in the Dreygaardian army and by the [[Vëlwathe]] as the patron of battle.

When [[The Sanguine Lord]] died, she went insane, and forced her hosts to gleefully murder everyone around them before finally killing themselves. For this reason, in modern academia her worship is commonly conflated with that of [[Octavian]].

Choler is commonly depicted as an angel surrounded in fire wielding a sword and axe, with a battle raging around her.
