###### Government
The Saran Houses make up the government of the [[Sara Dominion]], with each house having a responsibility to fulfil.
#### Major Houses
These houses were formed during the founding of the Sara Dominion, with [[Clio Sara]] instructing each of the [[Hunters of Sara]] to form a house in their name. These houses would then form the government of the empire, with her house being the ruling house.
- [[House Sara]]
- [[House Mentis]]
- [[House Orion]]
- [[House Meleager]]
- [[House Lerna]]
- [[House Erymr]]
- [[House Diana]]
- [[House Atalanta]]
- [[House Skadi]]
- [[House Nemea]]
- [[House Ceryn]]
- [[House Cadmus]]
#### Minor Houses
These houses were formed after the founding of the Sara Dominion. Some where formed in a similar fashion to the major houses, where the founder slayed a great chimaera and nobility was formed around their lineage, but some were formed in a more modern way, due to trade and personal influence.
- [[House Apis]]
- [[House Taurus]]
- [[House Cassalanter]]
- [[House Vess]]
- [[La Domus de Papillons]]
