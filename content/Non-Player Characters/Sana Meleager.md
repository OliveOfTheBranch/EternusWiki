###### (she/her) - NPC
Sana Meleager is the current head of [[House Meleager]]. 

Her older brother, [[Dalys Meleager]] was the previous head of House Meleager, and served as Regent of the [[Sara Dominion]] for 18 years. During this time, Sana lived in [[Cassowary]]. She is a mage, and is a high-ranking member of the [[Tears of Eurymede]].

Sana was the champion of House Meleager during King [[Theodore Sara]]'s coronation.
