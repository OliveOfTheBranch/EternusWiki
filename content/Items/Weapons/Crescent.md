###### Cardinal Armament
Crescent is a sword that was created by [[Yuphra]] to be their weapon in battle, alongside their hallowed lantern. When used by Yuphra, it was made of a silver so pure that it seemed to glow with moonlight.

When Yuphra imprisoned [[Týr Sara]] in chains for committing the sin of changing his own soul into [[Animus]] (via [[The Divine Rites#The Wolf's Ritual\|The Wolf's Ritual]]), they stabbed him in the head with Crescent. After Týr was freed by the [[Godeater]], the [[Cardinal]] magic corroded the blade, tarnishing it so black and dark that it no longer glowed.

The sword is still in the possession of Týr Sara.
