###### Country
Yupral is the largest country in [[Ethrys]], encompassing the whole western side of the continent, from the cold, northern [[Dreaming Wood]], all the way to the southern rainforests of [[Felor]]. 

Yuphral was named after the god, [[Yuphra]], who wove together the souls of mortals. It is home to the oldest human settlements, including the city of [[Crux]], as central Yuphral was the place where mortals were created. The early Yuphrali people spoke [[Draconic]], and worshipped Yuphra, as well as the other members of [[The Twelve Who Came Before]]. 

After [[Agon#The Second Agon|The Second Agon]], when Yuphra was slain by [[Alexandria]], the entire country underwent a massive religious upheaval. Churches and libraries were burned, as the Yuphrali people denounced Yuphra, and embraced Alexandria as their new god. This rejection of the old gods caused the [[Ashen Empire]] to form on the Ashen Islands, an area that was previously considered a part of Yuphral.
