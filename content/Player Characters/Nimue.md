###### (she/her) - Played by Olivia
Nimue is a human huntress from the [[Sara Dominion]]. She is the wielder of the bow [[Caliburn]], which was bound to her by [[Thaegan of Shrike]]. She was alive during the Early [[Golden Age]].

Her bow could be summoned via a [[Portae|Porta]] on her right arm, which could also be used to harness [[Fey]] magic.
