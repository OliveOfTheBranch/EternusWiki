###### City
Crux is a city in central [[Yuphral]], and is considered by most to be the oldest human city in [[Eternus]]. 

The city was built by early Yuphrali people, who worshipped the god [[Yuphra]]. In modern day, the citizens of Crux worship [[Alexandria]], and in fact destroyed many symbols of Yuphra and its worship after [[Agon#The Second Agon|The Second Agon]].
