###### (she/her) - NPC
Melania, or the Mother, is one of the [[Haemmangels]] in [[Dreygaard]]ian mythology. Her hosts were mainly mothers or pregnant women. She is the protector of fertility both for the people and the land.

When [[The Sanguine Lord]] died, she went insane, and forced her hosts to kill their children. The hosts would then run into the wilderness and starve themselves to death, or eat rotten food until they died of sickness.

Melania is commonly depicted as an angel holding a sheaf of wheat and breastfeeding a child.
