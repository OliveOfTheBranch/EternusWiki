###### (she/her) - NPC
Cil'ahn of Shrike is a [[Shrike]] [[Fey]] living in [[Oldegate]]. She married [[Favon Atalanta]], the current head of [[House Atalanta]], shortly after [[Agon#The Rending|The Rending]] ended. She is a member of [[La Domus de Papillons]], due to her mother once being [[Archfey]] of Shrike.

She wields an enchanted spear tipped with ornately layered [[Urstrix]] feathers called [[Chrysalis]], which was given to her by [[Xenevieve Mentis-Sara]].
