###### Age
The Burning Age is the second recorded age of [[Eternus]], coming after the [[Golden Age]], and lasting 214 years. The Age began with the [[Fall of Arkas]] and the displacement of [[Sigil]], followed by the [[Time of Strife]], a century of devastation that affected all of Eternus. [[Ethrys]] suffered mostly from crop failure, while [[Alfheim]] was subject to a variety of issues caused by the displacement of Sigil, and subsequent creation of [[Landfall]] that allowed monsters such as [[Nightshades]] to freely roam the continent. 

The Burning Age ended when [[Tasha]] performed the [[Rending Ritual]], creating an artificial [[Agon]] that began the [[Current Age]].
