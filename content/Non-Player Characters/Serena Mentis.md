###### (she/her) - NPC
Serena Mentis was an elf from the [[Sara Dominion]]. Serena was born in a small coastal town, shortly before [[Agon#The Second Agon\|The Second Agon]]. She married an elf, and had a son, [[Pontus Mentis]], who was only 8 at the time of the Agon. She was unable to read the future, and as such, her mother, [[Belladonna Mentis]], briefly became oracle after the Second Agon, ruling for 8 years in her old age, before giving the position to her son, Pontus.
