###### (she/her) - NPC
Phlox, also known as the Hiemal, is one of the [[Haemmangels]] in [[Dreygaard]]ian mythology. She is said to have dominion over winter, and is said to appear in snowstorms to guide unwary travellers to safety.

When [[The Sanguine Lord]] died, she went insane, and forced her hosts to kill themselves by stripping naked and walking into the cold, causing them to die of hypothermia.

Phlox is commonly depicted as an angel wearing a woollen coat and holding a dying lantern, surrounded by a vicious blizzard.
