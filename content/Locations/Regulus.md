###### City
Regulus is a city located in the southern savanna of the [[Sara Dominion]]. It was the home of [[Saint Nemea]], and is still to this day inhabited by [[House Nemea]]. It is also the home of [[House Taurus]], and part of the Bas'Taurus region.
