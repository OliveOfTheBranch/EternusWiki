###### (she/her) - NPC
Lliira of Three is the goddess of joy and celebration, and is one of the [[Three Sisters]]. She currently lives on the [[Isle of the Three Sisters]] and is married to [[Eitr Sara]]. She is also known as the Snake Charmer, the Joybringer, or Lliira Sara.

She is depicted as a young woman wearing a yellow dress, dancing in barefoot.

As one of the Three Sisters, she is the demigod child of [[Lathander]].
