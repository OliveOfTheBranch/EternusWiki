###### (she/her) - Played by Lexi
Beatrice Derjyre, commonly known by her moniker Tinsel, is a half-Elf from the [[Impious Alliance]], who grew up in [[Emperia]]. With her father being Farven Derjyre, she is a member of [[House Derjyre]] of [[Mædway]], but as a bastard was raised by her Emperian mother, Alice.

When she turned eighteen, Beatrice's father arranged for her to marry [[Edward Rotka]], a pairing that would hopefully bring House Derjyre into higher repute. Beatrice did not wish to be married, but no matter how hard she tried to dissuade her father, he would not budge. She decided that the only way to escape the marriage would be to run away. When the day finally came she packed and snuck out, tying up her hair with two strings of tinsel, which thus inspired her new name, Tinsel. Tinsel slowly tried to cover up any evidence that she was in House Derjyre, constantly moving around until the noble house did not know where to search and reluctantly gave up.

Tinsel is a follower of [[Lliira]], the goddess of joy, and worships her through public bardistry. She is a member of the adventuring party [[Zhetrics]]. After the Zhetrics retired, she moved back to [[Emperia]] to reunite with her mother and pursue the spreading of joy.
