###### (he/him) - NPC
Atlas Mentis was a human from the [[Sara Dominion]]. Nestor’s two sons, [[Xander Mentis]] and Atlas, were raised in [[Arkas]] just before [[Agon#The Second Agon\|The Second Agon]]. Xander, Atlas’s older brother, became oracle at a relatively young age. Atlas saw the future in the bright colours of the sunset, and would spend hours watching the sky.

He foresaw a new age for Arkas, and assumed this to be a sign of great scientific upheaval. Arkas was obliterated by [[The Triad]] during Xander’s reign, killing both brothers.

Atlas saw the future in the colours of the sunset.
