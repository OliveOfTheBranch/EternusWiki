###### Adventuring Party
Eternus Victoria were an adventuring party that formed just before [[Agon#The Rending\|The Rending]]. Its members included Queen [[Xenevieve Mentis-Sara]], [[Vuris Sanavaloth]], [[Silva]], and [[Kairo of Marwin]]. They met at the [[Witchlight Carnival]] in the [[Sara Dominion]], and banded together to find their missing companions. During their time together, they travelled through [[Prismeer]], and killed the three hags that had taken control of the realm while the archfey, [[Tasha|Zybilna]], was frozen. 

At the end of their travels, they freed Zybilna, who revealed herself to be [[Tasha]], Sovereign of [[The Court of Pain]]. Tasha used a ritual known as the [[Rending Ritual]] to artificially create an agon. 
After this point, Eternus Victoria were briefly separated, though reunited in [[Chimaera]], where they worked together to figure out how to end the Rending.
