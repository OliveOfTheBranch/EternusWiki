###### Noble House
House Mentis ([[Sylvan]]: "of the mind") are the official royal oracles to the empire. They are descended from the [[Non-Player Characters/Aureon Mentis\|Oracle of Sara]], from [[Arkas]], whose real name was lost to time, and [[Non-Player Characters/Britta of Shrike\|Brittany Mentis]]. They are the only oracle to have ever predicted an Agon before it happened. 

The current head of house and only living member of the line is [[Xenevieve Mentis-Sara]] (née Mentis). The status of the house now that they have married into the Sara line is unknown. 

House Mentis is based in [[Chimaera]].

Their symbol is a star and laurel wreath, and their words are "Something wicked this way comes."
