Portae are magical tattoos designed by [[Ulysses Sara]], [[Aureon Mentis]], and [[Eurymede]]. In early arcane magic, their job was to bend the [[Veil]] around the wielder's body such that energy could be moved through their portae and into material form. They resemble modern-day sigils, which are now written in books instead of placed upon the body.

Even after modern scholars have moved to spell books and glyphs, portae are still commonly used by [[Ossindra]] and the [[Tears of Eurymede]] to hone their arcane prowess.
