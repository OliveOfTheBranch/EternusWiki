###### (he/him) - NPC
Jarred is a human [[Werewolves|Werewolf]] bonesmith, and is part of [[The Pack]]. He created [[Veilpiercer]] for [[Xenevieve Mentis-Sara|Xenevieve Mentis]], as well as creating King [[Theodore Sara]]'s engagement ring out of [[Therizo]] bone. He also created the base weapons for [[Queen's Hunt]] and [[Chrysalis]], which were then enchanted by [[Cassandra Dannell]] and given as a gift from [[Xenevieve Mentis-Sara|Xenevieve Mentis]] to Lord [[Favon Atalanta]] and his wife Lady [[Cil'ahn of Shrike]].
