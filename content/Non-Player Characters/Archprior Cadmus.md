###### (she/her) - NPC
Archprior Cadmus was one of the [[Hunters of Sara]], and is responsible for the spread of [[The Priory]] into the [[Sara Dominion]]. Cadmus was born in [[Felor]], but was exiled from her home after slaying the [[Elder Dragons|Elder Dragon]], [[Thebes]]. She was also the first individual known to have performed the ritual of [[The Divine Rites#Vox Sowing|Vox Sowing]], giving her the ability to speak the [[Old Tongue]] fluently. She also drank blood from the heart of Thebes, allowing her to talk to birds.
