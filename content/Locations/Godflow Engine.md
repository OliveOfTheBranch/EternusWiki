The godflow engine is a colossal series of golden pylons surrounding [[Carceri]] that draw [[The Wanderer]]'s [[Animus]] from the prison plane and feed it to the flying city of [[Sigil]], which gives [[The Court of Pain]] their divine powers. Its interior is controlled by the Grazed Sovereign [[Carter of Marwin]], who ensures that the animus cannot leave, while the pylons themself are controlled by [[Aureon Mentis]], the Sovereign of Knowledge.
