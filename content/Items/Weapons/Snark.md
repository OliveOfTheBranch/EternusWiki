###### Cardinal Armament
Snark is a magical sword currently owned by [[Vuris Sanavaloth]]. 

The creature within the sword sharing its name was summoned from the [[Outer Realm]] by [[Titania]], and given physical form as a [[Fey]]. It once roamed the [[Feywild]] freely, and spent much time in [[Marwin]]. It is a [[Cardinal]] entity, so far beyond comprehension that it can only be seen outside of its sword form by young children, whose imaginations know no bounds. 

It was bound to a sword by [[Tasha]], and remained with her in the Palace of Heart's Desire in [[Prismeer]], until it was stolen by Vuris during [[Agon#The Rending|The Rending]].
