###### Pantheon
The Cloaked Gods are a subset of [[The Twelve Who Came Before]], comprising only of the gods who never or very rarely chose to take a physical avatar, and so instead were depicted as a figure wearing a cloak. These gods were:
- [[The Sanguine Lord]]
- [[Pölark]]
- [[The Shepherd]]
- [[Yuphra]]
- [[The Wanderer]]

In the earliest days, almost all gods were considered cloaked gods, due to them existing in their higher forms alone. However, as time went on, many chose to take a physical avatar in order to more easily communicate with their subjects.
