###### Noble House
House Cassalanter is one of the minor [[Saran Houses]], and is based in [[Archela]]. It also has a branch in [[Emperia]]. The house begun as a family of playwrights in Bas'Taurus who grew popular by basing their stories on exotic [[Fey]] fables. As a family, they worshipped [[The Sanguine Lord]] for many generations.

The Cassalanters lived in obscurity for many generations, until a stroke of unforeseen luck caused them to become rich and popular very quickly.

The current head of house in Emperia is [[Victoro Cassalanter]].

Their symbol is the masks of comedy and tragedy.
