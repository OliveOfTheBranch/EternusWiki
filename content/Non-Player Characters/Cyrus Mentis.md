###### (he/him) - NPC
Cyrus Mentis was an elf from the [[Sara Dominion]]. Cyrus’s older sister, [[Medora Mentis]], became oracle at 29, taking over from their mother, [[Arabella Mentis]]. They both spent much of their time in [[Drachma]], the home of their father, though occasionally visited [[Chimaera]] for business. His sister was the oracle in power during the trade of [[House Erymr]] with [[House Lerna]]. Cyrus ended up forming a good relationship with the newly changed House Lerna, using his visions to help with investments. He married a human and had a daughter, [[Cressida Mentis]], who eventually became oracle. 

Cyrus could fall into vivid prophetic visions through reading.
