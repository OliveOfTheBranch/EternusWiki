###### (she/her) - NPC
Princess Victoria Sara is a half-elf from the [[Sara Dominion]]. 

She is the youngest daughter of Queen [[Xenevieve Mentis-Sara]], and King [[Theodore Sara]], and the younger sister of Princess [[Anastasia Sara]]. She lives in the Royal Palace in [[Chimaera]], but is known to take frequent trips to [[Oldegate]] and [[Port Kal'Vess]] with her family. 

Victoria has wavy brown hair and hazel eyes. It is unknown whether she possesses any magical abilities.
