###### City
Öndur is a city in the Fenris region of [[Dreygaard]]. It was once a part of the [[Sara Dominion]], and was the home of [[House Skadi]], though eventually ended up in the hands of Dreygaard sometime during the [[Golden Age]] after being annexed by the country. The main religion of the city is now [[The Sanguine Lord]], though many still follow [[Non-Player Characters/Clio Sara\|Kleos]] (despite it being illegal) due to her prior influence there.
