###### Fey Domain
Shrike is a realm in the [[Feywild]]. The people of Shrike are [[Wild Fey]], and are known for their stark white hair and striking red eyes. They are well versed in combat, and choose their [[Archfey]] via dueling. Anyone that wishes to rule may challenge the current Archfey for their title, and fight to the death. This has led to a system of government that is in a state of constant change. 

Despite the changeable nature of the realm, it has maintained a close relationship with the [[Sara Dominion]] ever since the Archfey, [[Britta of Shrike]], created the fey crossing between Shrike and [[Oldegate]] in the early [[Golden Age]]. 

The descendents of the various Shrike Archfey are joined together by [[La Domus de Papillons]], a noble [[Saran Houses|Saran House]] in Oldegate. Despite belonging to different bloodlines, these fey are considered family under Shrike tradition.
