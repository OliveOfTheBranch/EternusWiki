Eternus is the name of the reality which [[The Twelve Who Came Before]] descended to, and is also commonly used as a shortening of Eternus Primordia in reference to Eternus's material plane.

Inside of Eternus, [[Primordeus]] energy writes the rules of reality, basing itself around the gods' [[Animus]]. The [[Veil]] around Eternus protects it from the [[Outer Realm]], an unspace of pure [[Cardinal]] energy where the Twelve Who Came Before and the [[Godeater]]s come from.
#### Eternus Primordia
Eternus Primordia is the material plane of Eternus, and is made up of three continental landmasses: [[Ethrys]], [[Alfheim]], and [[The Pitch]]. As the divine light of [[Celestia]] shines upon Eternus Primordia, its reflection forms the [[Feywild]], while its shadow forms the [[Shadowfell]].
