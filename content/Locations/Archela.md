###### City
Archela is a city in the Bas'Taurus region of the [[Sara Dominion]], and the oldest city in the country. It was founded by the scorpion [[Chimaera (Creatures)|Chimaera]]) general [[Antares]], who used its venom to poison the humans who lived there and mind-control them. Since [[Saint Orion]] slayed Antares and liberated the people of Archela, his descendents of [[House Orion]] have ruled the city as its guardian.

Archela contains the Bastion, the oldest still-standing library of [[Ethrys]] that were once the personal chambers of Antares. It now holds the secrets of House Orion, having been enchanted to become more labyrinthine the deeper it goes.
