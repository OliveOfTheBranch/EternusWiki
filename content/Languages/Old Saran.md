Old Saran is the language spoken by [[Typhon]] and his [[Creatures/Chimaera (Creatures)|Chimaera]] generals, and was later adopted by the people of the Hunting Grounds. It is the origin of modern [[Saran]] and [[Dreygaardian]], and is still spoken in the [[Sara Dominion]] during important speeches and ceremonies.
