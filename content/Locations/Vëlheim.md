###### Island Group
Vëlheim, or the Vëlheim Isles, is a group of islands northeast of [[Dreygaard]]. 

It is inhabited by the [[Vëllan]], a population that is indigenous to northern Dreygaard and the Vëlheim Isles. The Vëllan were created by [[The Wanderer]], and rarely stray from their ancestral home, despite the harsh conditions. The islands are also home to a variety of dangerous creatures, including [[Hags]], [[Vampires]], and [[Ols]]. Dreygaard have led many expeditions here to hunt the Vëllan, as they condemn them for witchcraft, an act of heresy against their god, [[The Sanguine Lord]].
