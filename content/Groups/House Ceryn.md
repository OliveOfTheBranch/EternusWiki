###### Noble House
House Ceryn is the head of foreign affairs in the [[Sara Dominion]]. They are descended from [[Saint Ceryn]], who was known as the nomadic hunter.

Their family artefact is the Fleet Ring. The current head of house is [[Ilya Ceryn]], who is a [[Creatures/Changelings\|Changeling]] raised in [[Chimaera]] that has acclimated to the family, giving him the blood of a Ceryn.

House Ceryn is based in [[Chimaera]].

Their symbol is a hind, and their words are "Let the prey become predator."
