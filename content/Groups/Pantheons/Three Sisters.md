###### Pantheon
The Three Sisters are a group of three demigod children of [[Lathander]], being [[Lliira]], [[Suné]], and [[Eldath]].

They represent, protect, and personify the universally good things of the world, such as joy, love, and peace. They live on the [[Isle of the Three Sisters]], protected by [[Eitr Sara]], who surround the island in the form of a huge serpent.

The three were trained as [[Archangels]] to bolster Lathander's troops during the [[Blood War]], but quickly gave up the path of war to care for the lives of mortals as the inverse of the [[Morteus Gods]].
