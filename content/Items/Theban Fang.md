###### Artefact
The Theban Fang is the artifact of [[House Cadmus]]. 

It was taken by [[Archprior Cadmus]] from the [[Elder Dragons|Elder Dragon]] [[Thebes]] that she slayed prior to [[Agon#The First Agon|The First Agon]]. This violent act caused her to be exiled from her home in [[Felor]], leading her to take up residence in the [[Hunting Grounds]], where she was eventually sought out by [[Clio Sara]], and joined the [[Hunters of Sara]]. 

The Theban Fang is used in a ritual known as [[The Divine Rites#Vox Sowing|Vox Sowing]], where the new head of House Cadmus must pierce the fang into their throat, and use the [[Old Tongue]] to stop the bleeding. If successful, the fang remains lodged in place, and the user is able to speak the Old Tongue with perfect fluency, though loses all other speech.
