###### Group
The Pack is an ancient group of lycanthropes (specifically [[Werewolves]]) led by [[Týr Sara]], the Fenrisúlfr. His deputies, and leaders of the Pack in his absence, are [[Hati]] and [[Sköll]], the Mooneater and Suneater respectively.
###### Notable Members
- [[Týr Sara]], founder, leader, and Lord of Wolves
- [[Hati]], deputy and ex-leader
- [[Sköll]], deputy and ex-leader
- [[Xenevieve Mentis-Sara]], ex-Duchess of Wolves, honourary
- [[Ylga]], honourary
- Jarred, Bonesmith
- Winnifred, Bonesmith
- Aedra
- Shanna
