###### Glaive
Iris is a glaive created by [[Iris Mentis]] and currently in the possession of [[Xenevieve Mentis-Sara]]. Its shaft is made out of Yuphrali redwood and the blade at its tip is a sliver of [[Sun Drakes|Sun Drake]] tooth, belonging to [[Siris]] Khan.

---
#### Glaive of Iris
*Weapon (glaive: 1d6 (2d4 two-handed) slashing damage, finesse, reach, heavy), very rare (requires attunement)*

You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

While holding this weapon with two hands, if you roll doubles on your damage dice, you can roll again and add that damage on top. This effect can stack until you don't roll a double.
