###### Noble House
House Sara are the royal house of the [[Sara Dominion]]. The first of the line was [[Clio Sara]], who upon slaying the god [[Typhon]] ("The Predator") ascended to become a goddess herself. A fragment of her power is said to run through their veins. 

Their family artefact is the Bone Crown, which was originally formed from Typhon's bones, and has since been added to by each monarch. The current ruling king and head of house is [[Theodore Sara]]. 

House Sara is based in [[Chimaera]].

Their symbol is a chimaera wearing a crown, and their words are "Blessed is the Hunt."
